InvestMatch Nepal is the first dedicated platform that brings together verified entrepreneurs and serious capital providers inside Nepal — replacing fragmented Facebook groups and personal networks with a professional, transparent, and admin-moderated marketplace.

Why we exist

Early-stage capital in Nepal is real but invisible. Founders struggle to find the right investors, and investors struggle to find vetted deals. We fix that.

Our promise

Every single profile on this platform — investor or entrepreneur — has been reviewed by a human. No bots. No fake identities. No time wasters.
