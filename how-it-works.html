How InvestMatch Works

Four simple steps. Built on trust. Designed for real outcomes in the Nepali market.

01

1. Sign up & verify

Choose Investor or Entrepreneur. Complete your profile and upload citizenship / company registration documents. Our team reviews within 48 hours.

02

2. Set your mandate

Investors define sectors, ticket size, and stage. Entrepreneurs publish their full pitch (problem, traction, ask). Smart suggestions begin immediately.

03

3. Express interest

Verified investors can send up to 10 interest requests per day. Entrepreneurs review the investor’s full profile and decide to accept or reject.

04

4. Connect & close

Upon acceptance, contact details are revealed to both parties. A match record is created. You take the conversation offline — we stay out of the way.
