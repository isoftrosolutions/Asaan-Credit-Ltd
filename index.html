Frequently Asked Questions

How does the platform ensure profiles are genuine? +
Every profile is manually reviewed by our analysts. We verify email, phone, and social accounts. Businesses also undergo document verification (GST, registration certificate).
When do contact details get shared? +
Contact info is revealed only when both parties express mutual interest. This prevents unsolicited outreach and protects your identity.
What types of transactions are supported? +
Full business sale, partial stake sale, investment, business loan, asset sale, franchise, and distributorship opportunities.
Is there a fee to use InvestMatch? +
Basic registration is free. Premium plans start at NPR 25,500. A 1% finder's fee applies post deal closure on paid plans.
