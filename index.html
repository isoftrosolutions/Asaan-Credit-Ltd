Frequently Asked Questions

How does the platform ensure profiles are genuine? +
Every profile is manually reviewed by our analysts. We verify email, phone, and social media accounts before activation. Businesses also undergo document verification (GST, registration certificate).
When do contact details get shared? +
Contact information is revealed only when both parties express mutual interest. This ensures confidentiality and prevents unsolicited outreach.
What types of transactions are supported? +
Full business sale, partial stake sale, investment, business loan, asset sale, franchise, and distributorship opportunities.
Is there a fee to use InvestMatch? +
Basic registration is free. Premium subscription plans start at NPR 25,500 for businesses and investors. A 1% finder's fee applies post deal closure on paid plans.
