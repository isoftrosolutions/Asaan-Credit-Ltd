Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

1. Eligibility

Only Nepali citizens or Nepal-registered companies may create accounts. All users must pass manual verification.

2. Verification & Data

Verification documents (citizenship, PAN, company registration) are stored securely and only accessible to platform administrators for review purposes. They are never shared publicly.

3. Interest Requests & Matches

Contact information is only revealed after a mutual match. Users may not spam or misuse the interest system (daily limits enforced).

4. Privacy

We do not sell user data. Email and phone are used only for platform notifications and match facilitation. Users may request data deletion by contacting support.
