Support & Frequently Asked Questions

How long does verification take?

Our team aims to review all documents within 48 hours (business days). You will receive email notification.

Can international investors join?

No — v1 is restricted to Nepal citizens and Nepal-registered entities only (per PRD).

I need help with my pitch or profile.

Email support@investmatch.com.np or use the in-app support form after logging in.
